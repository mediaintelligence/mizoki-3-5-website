The problem

Organizations don't struggle because they lack data. They struggle because they cannot consistently determine what changed, why it changed, what should happen next, who should approve it, and whether it worked. Dashboards report; spreadsheets speculate; meetings decide; nobody remembers why.

What MIZ OKI Media is

A governed decision system for commercial performance — a new operating category: Causal Growth Control.

It connects commercial signals, causal reasoning, financial constraints, operational policy, human authority, and outcome learning into a single governed decision pathway. Every decision travels the same lifecycle and leaves the same inspectable record.

SENSE → REASON → PLAN → VALIDATE → DECIDE → ACT → LEARN

What makes it different

  • Diagnoses why performance moved — competing hypotheses tested on evidence, eliminated ones preserved
  • Compares counterfactual actions, always including doing nothing
  • Enforces your own policy — margin floors, budget ceilings, brand and risk rules — before anything is recommended
  • Routes every decision to its correct authority; autonomy exists only where explicitly granted
  • Executes only bounded, reversible, authorized actions — restraint recorded like action
  • Scores every prediction against its realized outcome — confidence is a track record, not a feeling

The way in

A 90-day pilot: observe-only for thirty days, human-reviewed recommendations for thirty, narrowly scoped governed actions for thirty — with agreed success metrics and explicit exit criteria. Autonomy is earned, never assumed.

Honesty discipline: every capability is labeled Live, Pilot-ready, Illustrative, or Roadmap — and illustrative numbers are never presented as verified production performance. house rule
