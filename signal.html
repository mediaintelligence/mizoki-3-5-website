// Intent stages

Where every person and account stands — continuously scored.

ORACLE classifies intent into four stages and keeps the score current as micro-signals arrive. Marketers subscribe to transitions — "notify when this account enters in-market" — instead of discovering them a quarter late.

STAGE 01 Awareness

Early, exploratory interest. Broad topics, shallow dwell. Spend stays light.

STAGE 02 Consideration

Interest is narrowing. PRECEDES-edge patterns begin matching known paths to purchase.

STAGE 03 In-market

A buying window is opening. Concentrate acquisition spend here — earlier than competitors can see.

STAGE 04 Purchase-imminent

Timing prediction says the decision is near. Time outreach to receptivity, not to a fixed cadence.

// The proof half

Causal measurement, stress-tested before it's trusted.

Uplift meta-learners — X-Learner and DR-Learner — estimate who is persuadable, not just who converts. Then every causal estimate faces DoWhy refutation: placebo-treatment, random-common-cause, and data-subset tests. An estimate that fails refutation is flagged, not shipped.

Holdout

Withhold media from a randomized control group; compare against the exposed group. The classic design — clean, direct, budget-hungry.

Ghost-bid

Log the auction instances where a control user would have been served the ad — identifying the counterfactual without spending on placebo ads. The most efficient control-group design in modern platforms.

Geo

Run a channel in test markets, hold it out of statistically matched control markets. The rigorous way to measure broadly-targeted, hard-to-address channels — CTV, social prospecting, paid search.

// The caused-vs-anticipated credit ledger

Every conversion in the ledger is classified — with a confidence interval — as one of two things:

CAUSED — marketing produced a net-new outcome, proven by experiment ANTICIPATED — ORACLE predicted the customer would convert anyway

This is what turns intent prediction from a liability ("you're taking credit for demand that already existed") into an asset: here is exactly what we caused, and here is what we would have gotten for free. Spend that only claims credit is the first budget to cut — lowering CAC without lowering volume.

Platform-reported ROAS credits any conversion after exposure. Incremental ROAS reflects only net-new revenue proven by experiment — and public geo-test data routinely shows platform numbers overstating measured iROAS, widest on brand search and retargeting. "Proven-incremental" is a term we use only after an experiment passes refutation.
