§01 · The measurement gap

Reported ROAS is a claim. Incremental ROAS is a finding.

What platforms report

A conversion that followed an impression. The platform selected the audience, served the ad, and then graded its own work. Brand search and retargeting look strongest here precisely because they reach people who were already coming.

What Signal measures

The difference between customers who saw the ad and statistically identical customers who did not. That difference is the only part of the number your budget created — and it is routinely a fraction of what was reported.

Correlation-based intent vendors have the same problem in a different coat: a surge score tells you an account is researching. It cannot tell you whether your response changed the outcome.

§02 · The proof engine

Four stages between a hunch and a finding.

Signal does not score attribution and stop. It designs the experiment, estimates the effect, then actively tries to break its own conclusion before that conclusion is allowed to move money.

  1. Design the counterfactual

    Register the experiment before the first impression: a randomized holdout, ghost bids that log the auctions a control customer would have won, or matched control geographies for channels that can't be held out per person. Minimum detectable effect is declared up front, not discovered afterward.

  2. Estimate who was actually persuadable

    Uplift modeling separates customers your marketing moved from customers who were always going to convert — and from those it annoyed. Effects are estimated per segment and per region, because a channel that works in one market often does nothing in another.

  3. Try to break the result

    Every estimate faces automated refutation: swap the real treatment for a placebo and the effect must collapse; inject a random confounder and the estimate must not move; re-run on data subsets. A result that survives is reportable. A result that fails is flagged, not shipped.

  4. Write the ledger

    Each conversion is classified caused or anticipated, with lift and a confidence interval, and written to the immutable audit trail. Budget reallocation reads from this ledger — never from platform-reported ROAS alone.

§04 · On the roadmap

Anticipatory intent Preview · in development

Signal is being extended to read emerging intent from consented behavioral signals — the same candidate-generation and ranking architecture the major platforms use — and to classify where a customer sits on the path from awareness to purchase-imminent.

Predicted next interest

Calibrated probabilities, not affinity labels. A 70% prediction is designed to be right about 70% of the time, and is measured for that.

Explainable by construction

Every prediction returns the path through the interest graph that produced it. No score is delivered without its reasoning.

Married to the ledger

Predicted intent never claims credit on its own. Acting on it requires a registered holdout, so anticipation is never mistaken for causation.

Stated plainly: this is anticipatory modeling of consented behavior, not mind-reading, and it is in development rather than deployed. Signal uses no microphone or audio signals of any kind — not now, not on the roadmap.

§05 · Governance

The constraints are the product.

Consent decides ingestion

Behavioral signals without a matching consent scope are never stored. The check runs before persistence, and it fails closed.

No audio. Ever.

Microphone and voice-derived targeting is refused at the schema level. It is not a setting an operator can switch on.

Sensitive categories are refused

Health, sexuality, religion, financial distress and minors are excluded from inference, storage and activation — regardless of model confidence.

Your customers keep their rights

Intent scoring is profiling under GDPR and is treated that way: access, correction, objection and erasure that cascades through every store.

Marketing organizations increasingly have to answer for how a number was produced. Signal is built so the answer is short.
