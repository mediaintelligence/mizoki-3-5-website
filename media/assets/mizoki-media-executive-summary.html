Causal Growth Control · powered by the MIZ OKI Decision Graph

The problem
The most expensive commercial decisions — budget moves, incident responses, promotion calls — are still made through disconnected dashboards, spreadsheets, intuition, and meetings. Nobody can consistently say what changed, why, what to do, who approves, and whether it worked.
The category
Causal Growth Control — not a dashboard, not attribution, not a CDP, not bid automation. A governed decision system that starts where those tools stop.
The differentiator
The MIZ OKI Decision Graph preserves the complete lifecycle of every decision: evidence, context, reasoning, policy, approval, action, realized outcome — connected, inspectable, replayable. It remembers why.
How it operates
One lifecycle on every decision: SENSE → REASON → PLAN → VALIDATE → DECIDE → ACT → LEARN. Competing causes tested on evidence; counterfactuals compared including doing nothing; policy enforced before recommendation; authority explicit; actions bounded and reversible; predictions scored against outcomes.
Governance stance
Trust comes from governance, not automation. Observe-only by default; autonomy only where explicitly granted; named approvers; recorded vetoes; human rollback authority at all times.
Proof discipline
Every capability labeled Live, Pilot-ready, Illustrative, or Roadmap. Illustrative numbers are never presented as verified production performance. No compliance-certification claims.
The way in
A 90-day pilot: observe (days 1–30), recommend under human review (31–60), act within narrow grants (61–90). Agreed metrics, governance checkpoints, and three explicit exits — expand, extend, or stop. Whichever exit, you keep the evidence.
